# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

ForgeFlow is a university team platform built and operated by a small, dedicated
community of students, engineers, and educators. To keep the project healthy and
welcoming, everyone who participates — whether contributing code, filing issues,
reviewing pull requests, or joining discussions — agrees to uphold the guidelines
in this document.

We are committed to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in all discussions and reviews.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on the *work* — the code, the behaviour, the evidence — not the person.
- Keep project data, student information, and any private material confidential and
  handled according to the project's security and data rules.
- Credit others' ideas and acknowledge AI-assisted contributions transparently.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Knowingly introducing secrets, personal data, or third-party material without the
  required licences and approvals.
- Deliberately degrading CI, safety checks, or legal gates, or bypassing them to
  sneak changes through.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable
behaviour and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in
response to any behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or
harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — including repositories,
issues, pull requests, discussions, and project meetings — and also applies when an
individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported
by contacting the project maintainers (the repository owners listed in the README).
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and confidentially, and a
response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances will be given.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face
temporary or permanent consequences as determined by the project leadership.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct was written specifically for the ForgeFlow project, drawing on
common community standards adapted to this project's academic and governed-team
context.
