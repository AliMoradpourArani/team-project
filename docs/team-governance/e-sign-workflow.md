# ForgeFlow Online Signing Workflow

Current governing-law baseline: **laws of the Islamic Republic of Iran**.

ForgeFlow uses a two-layer evidence model:

1. a repository-native **ForgeFlow Legal Gate** required before a non-exempt contributor can merge code;
2. a dedicated e-signature provider for higher-risk engagements where stronger identity/signature evidence is appropriate.

## Repository-native Legal Pack

The versioned Legal Pack lives under `.github/legal/` and includes the Developer/IP/Confidentiality Agreement, Engineering Access & Security Policy, Pre-Existing IP Disclosure and Offboarding acknowledgement.

Before merge, a non-exempt contributor must accept the current Legal Pack from the same GitHub account that authored the PR and declare either:

- `Pre-Existing IP: NONE`, or
- `Pre-Existing IP: DISCLOSED SEPARATELY`.

The second path remains pending until `@HoosseinRahimi` confirms the separate disclosure through the exact version/user-specific owner-approval comment generated by the Legal Gate.

Do not place confidential disclosure details, identity documents, compensation data or signed HR documents in GitHub.

## Recommended onboarding sequence

1. Collect only the minimum onboarding information needed for the engagement.
2. Determine the relationship classification under Iranian law and complete any required employment/services documentation.
3. Give the developer access only to the minimum systems needed, normally GitHub **Write** for Core development.
4. The developer creates a branch and opens a PR.
5. The ForgeFlow Legal Gate presents the current versioned Legal Pack and blocks merge until the required acknowledgement is recorded.
6. If pre-existing IP exists, collect its details privately and provide the owner approval only after that record is reviewed.
7. Require normal CI, Security and CODEOWNER review in addition to `ForgeFlow Legal Gate`.
8. For higher-value or sensitive engagements, send the full documents through an approved e-signature provider as an additional evidence layer.
9. Prefer a signing process that supports signer identification, proof of intent/control, tamper detection, retained date/time and an audit trail.
10. Save signed documents and audit trails in the private legal/document system, not GitHub.
11. Review access whenever role/scope changes.
12. At termination/end of access, revoke permissions, rotate affected credentials and complete the offboarding return/deletion record.

## Iran electronic-signature notes

Iran's Electronic Commerce Law recognizes electronic signatures and electronic records, with stronger evidential treatment available where the secure-signature/secure-record requirements are met. A GitHub acceptance comment is useful authenticated electronic evidence but is not represented by ForgeFlow as automatically satisfying every higher secure-signature standard.

For sensitive IP, disputed worker classification, equity arrangements, high compensation or other higher-risk engagements, retain a dedicated e-signature certificate/audit trail in addition to the GitHub Legal Gate record.

See `iran-legal-basis.md`, `legal-onboarding-checklist.md`, and `.github/legal/README.md`.

## Future incorporation

When a ForgeFlow company is incorporated, execute the founder-to-company IP transfer, update future Legal Pack documents to name the company directly and preserve historical acceptance/signature records as chain-of-title evidence.
