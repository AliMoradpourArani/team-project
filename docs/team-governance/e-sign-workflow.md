# ForgeFlow Online Signing Workflow

Recommended sequence for each new private-Core developer:

1. Collect only the minimum onboarding information needed for the engagement.
2. Complete the Developer/IP/Confidentiality Agreement and confirm the governing law/jurisdiction.
3. Complete the Pre-Existing IP disclosure and any approved third-party exceptions.
4. Send the documents through the approved e-signature provider.
5. Save the final signed documents and signature audit trail in the private legal/document system, not GitHub.
6. Record agreement version/date and access authorization.
7. Grant the minimum GitHub/system permissions needed, normally GitHub **Write** for Core development.
8. Require branch + PR + CI + CODEOWNER review for changes to `main`.
9. Review access whenever role/scope changes.
10. At termination/end of access, revoke permissions, rotate affected credentials, and complete the Offboarding Return/Deletion Certificate.

## Future incorporation

When a ForgeFlow company is incorporated, execute the founder-to-company IP transfer, update future agreements to name the company as the recipient, and preserve the historical signed agreements as chain-of-title evidence.
