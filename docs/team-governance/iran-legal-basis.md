# Iran legal basis for ForgeFlow contributor onboarding

This repository uses the laws of the Islamic Republic of Iran as the intended governing-law baseline for the current pre-incorporation ForgeFlow contributor agreement. This document is an operational legal reference, not legal advice.

## Contract validity

The agreement is intended to operate consistently with the Iranian Civil Code, including:

- Article 10: private contracts are binding when they do not conflict with explicit law.
- Article 190: validity requires intention and consent, capacity, a defined subject matter, and lawful purpose.
- Article 219: lawfully concluded contracts are binding on the parties.

## Software rights

The ForgeFlow agreement distinguishes economic rights from moral rights under Iran's software-protection regime.

- Transferable economic rights include use, publication, supply, execution, reproduction and economic exploitation.
- For qualifying commissioned software, the implementing regulation allocates economic rights to the commissioning party for the statutory period unless the parties agree to a shorter or more limited arrangement.
- Moral rights, including attribution, remain with the software creator and are non-transferable. ForgeFlow therefore does not rely on a blanket moral-rights assignment or waiver.

## Electronic signatures

The Electronic Commerce Law recognizes electronic signatures and electronic records. For stronger evidential value, ForgeFlow should use an e-signature process that:

- uniquely identifies the signatory,
- records the signatory's intent,
- makes later changes detectable,
- preserves the final document/data message,
- retains date/time and audit evidence.

Signed agreements and audit trails stay in a private legal-records system, never in this source repository.

## Employment boundary

If a contributor is in substance a worker/employee, Iranian Labour Law may apply regardless of the label used in the IP agreement. The Developer/IP/Confidentiality Agreement supplements, and does not replace, any required employment contract or mandatory labour protections.

## Future incorporation

Until a company is formed, Hossein Rahimi is the contractual recipient of allocated/assigned ForgeFlow economic rights. After incorporation, execute a founder-to-company IP assignment and update future contributor agreements to name the company directly.

## Primary references

- Civil Code of the Islamic Republic of Iran
- Electronic Commerce Law (1382 / 2003)
- Act on Protection of Rights of Computer Software Creators (1379 / 2000)
- Implementing Regulation for Articles 2 and 17 of the software-protection act
- Labour Law (1369 / 1990)

Before first production use, have an Iranian lawyer review the signed agreement template, especially employment classification, dispute resolution, software-right allocation, and any invention/patent clauses.
